Noughts & Crosses: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Noughts & Crosses ("the app") is a free game published by Striped Ape. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no internet permission.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your sound and vibration preferences on your own device so it remembers them. This information never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Crash reports

If you have opted in through your Android device settings, Google may share anonymous crash and performance data with us through Google Play. This is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
